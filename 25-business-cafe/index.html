Our Story

Started with one roaster, in a garage.

Fernweh opened in 2019 after our founder, Ines, spent three years roasting coffee in her garage for anyone who'd try it. We still roast every bag in-house, in small batches, sourced directly from three family farms we visit every year.

No syrups, no gimmicks — just good beans, treated carefully.
