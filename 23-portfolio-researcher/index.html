About

From physics to public health.

I started in statistical physics before moving into computational social science, drawn by questions no single field could answer alone. My PhD (Oxford, 2018) modeled epidemic spread on social networks; my current work extends that to how false information spreads the same way — and how accurate information can be designed to spread faster.

I collaborate closely with public health agencies to turn network models into interventions that actually get deployed.
