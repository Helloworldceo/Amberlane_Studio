About

Twelve years, three novels, one obsession.

I spent a decade as an archivist before writing my first novel — a habit that hasn't left my fiction since. Each of my books starts with a real, minor historical figure and asks what their interior life might have been.

I live outside Edinburgh, where I'm at work on a fourth novel set during the 1918 Spanish flu.
