About

Digital brush, analog brain.

I've been drawing professionally for 8 years — first as an animator, now as a full-time illustrator. My work leans bright, a little chaotic, and always a little funny. I love drawing food, creatures, and people mid-laugh.

When a brief says "make it feel human," that's usually when I do my best work.
