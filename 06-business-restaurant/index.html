Our Story

Cooking the way our grandparents did.

Olive & Ember started as a single wood-fired oven in a backyard, built by chef Teo Marchetti after years spent cooking across Greece and southern Italy. Today, that same oven anchors our kitchen — everything from our bread to our branzino passes through live fire.

We source produce from local farms, change our menu with the seasons, and believe good food should never be complicated.
