FAQ

Common questions

Is Flowly free?

Yes — the core app is free forever. Flowly Pro adds unlimited habits and advanced insights for $4.99/mo.

Does it work offline?

Yes, all check-ins work offline and sync automatically once you're back online.

Can I export my data?

Yes, export everything as CSV any time from Settings → Export Data.

Is my data private?

Your habit data is never sold or shared. Accountability circles are opt-in and only show what you choose to share.
