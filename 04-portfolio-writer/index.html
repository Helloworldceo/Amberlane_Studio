About

Hi, I'm Sam.

I'm a freelance journalist and essayist based in Chicago, writing about the intersection of technology, work, and everyday life. My reporting has appeared in The Atlantic, Wired, The Guardian, and Bloomberg CityLab.

Before going freelance, I spent six years as a staff reporter covering the tech industry. These days I split my time between longform journalism, ghostwriting, and a slow-going book about the future of work.
