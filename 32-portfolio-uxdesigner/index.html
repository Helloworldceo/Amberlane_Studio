Case Studies

Selected work

Ledger — Redesigning reconciliation

B2B Fintech · 0→1 redesign · 6 months

ProblemReconciliation took finance teams 3+ hours a week, spread across 4 disconnected views.
ApproachConsolidated the workflow into one view, prototyped with 8 finance users across 3 rounds.
OutcomeAverage reconciliation time dropped to 40 minutes; NPS for the module rose 22 points.

Northwind — Onboarding overhaul

B2B SaaS · Growth · 3 months

Problem62% of new accounts churned before completing setup.
ApproachRebuilt onboarding as a guided checklist with progressive disclosure, cut required fields by half.
OutcomeSetup completion rose from 38% to 71%; activation-to-paid conversion up 18%.

Fieldpoint — A design system from scratch

Design Systems · Internal tooling · 4 months

ProblemInconsistent UI across 5 product teams slowed every release.
ApproachBuilt a token-based system in Figma + React, working with 3 engineering leads.
OutcomeFeature build time down 30%; design QA bugs down 45% in the first quarter.
