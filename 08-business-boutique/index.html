Our Story

Started on a kitchen table.

Marigold & Co. began in 2016 as a small pop-up market stall, selling ceramics made by our founder, Alice, and a handful of friends. A decade later, we're a full storefront working with over 30 independent makers across the country.

We still believe in the same thing we did back then: buy less, choose well, and support the people who make things by hand.
