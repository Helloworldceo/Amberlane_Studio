FAQ

Common questions

Can I customize any of the templates?

Yes — every template on this site can be used as a starting point. We'll swap in your branding, content, images, and adjust the structure to fit your business.

What if I don't see a style I like?

No problem — mention that in your request and we'll design something fully custom for your brand instead.

How long does a project take?

Template customizations typically take about a week. Fully custom builds usually take 2–4 weeks depending on scope.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

We can — our Ongoing Care plan includes hosting, domain management, and monthly updates. Or we can hand off a ready-to-deploy site if you'd rather manage it yourself.

I'm not ready to commit — can I just ask questions first?

Of course. Use the request form below and mention you're just exploring — no pressure, no obligation.
