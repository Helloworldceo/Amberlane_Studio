Studio

Quiet, material-led design.

I trained as an architect but I've always cared more about how a room feels at 7am than how it photographs. My studio takes on a small number of projects a year — houses, additions, and a handful of restaurants and small hotels — so every one gets full attention.

Material honesty, daylight, and proportion do most of the work. Decoration comes last, if at all.
