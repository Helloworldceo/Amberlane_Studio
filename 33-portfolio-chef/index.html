About

Ten years of restaurant kitchens, now yours.

I spent a decade cooking in restaurant kitchens — the last four as sous chef at a one-Michelin-star kitchen — before deciding I wanted to cook for smaller rooms, where I could actually talk to the people eating.

Every menu is built around what's in season that week and adjusted for your kitchen, your guests, and any dietary needs.
