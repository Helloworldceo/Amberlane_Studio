About

Songs built to be played loud in small rooms.

Wren Calloway is a singer-songwriter from Asheville, NC, blending folk storytelling with the grit of alt-country. Three albums deep and a decade of touring dive bars and festival stages alike, Wren's live show is built on the same thing every record is: honesty over polish.

Lowlight, Wren's third full-length, was recorded live to tape with a five-piece band over two weeks in an old chapel.
