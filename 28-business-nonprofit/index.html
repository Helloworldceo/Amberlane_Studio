Our Mission

Access shouldn't be a lottery.

Bright Path was founded in 2014 after our co-founders spent a year documenting the gap between well-funded and under-funded early-learning programs. What we found: the difference wasn't ambition or need — it was access.

Today we partner with local communities to build, staff, and sustain early-learning centers that are run by and for the people who live there.
