Our Story

Baked before sunrise, every day.

Little Wren started in 2019 when Mei, a former pastry chef, began selling croissants out of a farmers market tent. Three years later, we opened our storefront on Oak Street — same recipes, same 4am starts.

We bake in small batches, source our flour from a mill two hours away, and pull our espresso from beans roasted a mile from our door.
