The Space

Built in 1924. Restored in 2021.

Originally a union hall, The Gable Room kept its original timber trusses, brick walls, and 22-foot ceilings through a two-year restoration. We added modern lighting, climate control, and a full catering kitchen — everything else is exactly as it was.

CapacityUp to 180 seated, 240 standing
